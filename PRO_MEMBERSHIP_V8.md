# EIT PRO Membership V8

- 2 skills: 99,000đ/month
- 3 skills: 129,000đ/month
- 4 skills: 179,000đ/month
- Course registrants are auto-granted 4-skill PRO for 30 days; additional course registrations extend the entitlement.
- PRO purchase requests are saved to PostgreSQL in `pro_orders` for admin follow-up.
- Admin has a PRO orders panel.
- Course thumbnails are image placeholders in `course-thumbnails/`; replace them later or upload a poster through Admin → Courses.
- Team arrows use SVG and support click + horizontal drag/scroll.
